Research Preparation

Preparing to explore risk, water, and resilience through engineering.

Learning and Mentorship · Not Yet Formal Research

I am currently in the preparation stages of research. Through mentorship and focused learning, I am learning the technical skills needed to support future work in engineering, environmental systems, and catastrophe resilience. My goal is to use these skills to become a helpful and reliable contributor to future research projects.

Mentorship

I am currently being mentored by Ahmmed Rahman, a PhD student working under Dr. Hong-Yi Li at the Center of Catastrophe Modeling and Resilience. This mentorship is helping me understand how technical skills can support research questions involving hazards, water, and resilient communities. In the future, I hope to assist with learning project methods, organizing information, and supporting the technical work behind research.

Coding

I am learning coding as a tool for organizing information, automating repeatable tasks, exploring models, and building a stronger foundation for computational engineering work. In future projects, coding may help me clean data, automate workflows, and support simulations or other technical tasks.

Artificial Intelligence

I am exploring how AI can support research workflows, pattern recognition, technical analysis, and decision-making while continuing to learn how to evaluate results carefully and responsibly. In the future, I hope to help explore patterns in complex data and support tools that make technical work more efficient.

Data Analysis

I am developing the ability to prepare, interpret, and communicate data so that engineering questions can be approached with evidence, clear assumptions, and reproducible reasoning. In future research, I hope to help prepare datasets, identify trends, create visualizations, and communicate results.

Hydrology

I am beginning to learn how water moves through natural and built environments, including the role of precipitation, runoff, drainage, and watersheds in engineering and community resilience. This will help me contribute to future work involving water systems, environmental data, and hazard impacts.

Catastrophe Modeling

I am being introduced to how hazards and their potential impacts can be represented using data, probability, and models. This area connects my interest in civil engineering with risk awareness and resilient infrastructure. As I continue learning, I hope to help analyze risk, understand possible impacts, and support better-informed decisions about resilient communities.

Future contribution

As I build these skills, I hope to support future research by learning new methods, handling data carefully, assisting with coding and analysis, and contributing to work that improves understanding of hazards, water systems, and resilience.
