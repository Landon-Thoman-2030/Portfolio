Hobbies & Interests

Curiosity, time outdoors, and a commitment to keep learning.

Personal Interests

Outside of academics, I enjoy being active outdoors, learning from the past, and exploring ideas about people and the way we understand the world.

Camping & Hiking

Camping and hiking give me time to explore new places, stay active, and build the patience and self-reliance that come from spending time outdoors.

Eagle Scout

Earning the rank of Eagle Scout strengthened my leadership, service, teamwork, planning, and outdoor skills that I continue to value in school, engineering, and everyday life.

History

I enjoy learning about history to understand how people, societies, and major events have shaped the world and continue to influence the present.

Philosophy

Philosophy interests me because it encourages careful questions about knowledge, ethics, meaning, and how people reason through difficult ideas.

Psychology

I like learning about psychology to better understand human behavior, decision-making, motivation, and the ways people think and interact.

Genealogy

I enjoy exploring genealogy and family history to learn more about my family’s background and the people and stories that came before me.
