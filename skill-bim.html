Skill

BIM

Building Information Modeling

Building Information Modeling, or BIM, helps connect design, coordination, and project planning into a shared digital model. It supports better communication among stakeholders and improves understanding of how building systems work together. I have a working knowledge of Revit and understand the basics of BIM workflows and model-based design.

Core Strengths

  • Basic Revit knowledge
  • 3D model coordination
  • Spatial understanding
  • Project visualization
  • Collaborative planning

Why It Matters

  • Improves communication in design teams
  • Supports informed project decisions
  • Useful in civil and construction workflows
  • Helps manage complexity in systems
  • Builds understanding of model-based design
