Course

Academic and Analytical Writing

Communication and Evidence-Based Reasoning

This course helps develop clear, structured, and persuasive writing. It emphasizes analysis, argumentation, and the ability to present complex ideas in an organized and professional way. These skills are essential in both academic work and future engineering communication.

Skills Developed

  • Thesis-driven writing
  • Research and source analysis
  • Argument structure
  • Editing and revision

Why It Matters

  • Builds strong communication habits
  • Supports technical writing later in engineering
  • Improves critical thinking
  • Helps explain ideas to broader audiences
