Course

Calculus 1

Foundational Analytical Thinking

Calculus 1 is a foundational course that strengthens analytical reasoning, problem-solving, and the ability to model change in real systems. It provides the mathematical foundation that supports many engineering disciplines, especially when analyzing rates, motion, curves, and optimization.

What I’m Learning

  • Limits and continuity
  • Derivatives and applications
  • Optimization and modeling
  • Functions and graph interpretation

Why It Matters

  • Improves quantitative reasoning
  • Supports engineering analysis
  • Builds tools used in physics and design
  • Develops abstract problem-solving skills
