Course

Introductory Physics 1

Mechanics and Physical Systems

Introductory Physics 1 introduces the principles of motion, force, energy, and mechanics. It connects mathematical reasoning with physical behavior and helps build the analytical habits needed for future engineering courses and design challenges.

Topics Covered

  • Kinematics and motion
  • Forces and Newton’s laws
  • Energy and momentum
  • Applications of mechanics

Why It Matters

  • Builds engineering intuition
  • Connects math to physical systems
  • Supports structural and civil reasoning
  • Improves problem-solving under constraints
