Course

Integrated Engineering Practice

Engineering Design and Collaboration

Integrated Engineering Practice introduces students to the collaborative and design-based nature of engineering. The course focuses on applying engineering thinking in realistic problem-solving contexts, emphasizing teamwork, communication, and practical decision-making.

Learning Focus

  • Team-based engineering work
  • Design thinking
  • Problem framing
  • Project communication

Why It Matters

  • Builds practical engineering habits
  • Encourages real-world problem solving
  • Improves collaboration skills
  • Introduces the team process behind engineering work
